Go Service - is a professional tool designed for service industry specialists and service-based businesses. The app helps organize your workflows by combining schedule management, client database maintenance, and financial tracking into a single platform. With this app, you can plan appointments, save client contacts and their individual preferences, as well as manage your price list and service catalog. Built-in statistics allow you to track your income and workload, while the inventory management system helps you monitor your stock of work materials. The core purpose of the app is to bring all essential data into one place, making your daily work more structured and efficient. We are continuously developing the app and are always open to feedback to make the service even more beneficial for your business.
